Graph Databases

Databases optimized for graph-structured data. Native graph traversal is orders of magnitude faster than SQL JOINs for connected data.

Key Options

DatabaseQuery LanguageBest For
Neo4jCypherMost popular, general purpose

Cypher Example

Cypher // Find friends of friends who work at the same company MATCH (me:Person {name: "Alice"})-[:FRIENDS]->(friend)-[:FRIENDS]->(fof) WHERE (fof)-[:WORKS_AT]->(:Company {name: "TechCorp"}) RETURN DISTINCT fof.name

For Agentic AI

Graph databases power knowledge graph agents. Multi-hop reasoning (finding connections 2-3 levels deep) is natural in graph databases but very expensive in relational databases.
