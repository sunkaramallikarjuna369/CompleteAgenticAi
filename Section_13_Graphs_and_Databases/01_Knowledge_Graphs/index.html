What is a Knowledge Graph?

A structured representation of knowledge as entities (nodes) and relationships (edges).

(Albert Einstein) --[born_in]--> (Germany) (Albert Einstein) --[developed]--> (Theory of Relativity) (Theory of Relativity) --[field]--> (Physics) (Germany) --[located_in]--> (Europe)

Components

Entities

People, places, concepts, objects - the nodes

Relationships

Typed, directed connections between entities

Properties

Attributes on entities and relationships

Ontology

Schema defining valid types and relationships

Building Knowledge Graphs

  1. Entity Extraction: NER to identify entities
  2. Relation Extraction: Identify relationships
  3. Entity Resolution: Merge duplicates
  4. Knowledge Fusion: Combine sources
  5. Validation: Ensure consistency

For Agentic AI

Knowledge graphs enable structured reasoning. Agents traverse graphs to find connections and answer complex multi-hop questions that vector search alone cannot handle.
