Graph Neural Networks

Neural networks for graph-structured data. They learn representations by aggregating information from neighboring nodes.

Key Architectures

GCN

Graph Convolution. Aggregate 1-hop neighbors per layer.

GAT

Graph Attention. Weight neighbors by importance.

GraphSAGE

Sample and aggregate for scalability.

Graph Transformer

Transformer attention on graph nodes. State-of-the-art.

Applications

  • Social network analysis and community detection
  • Molecular property prediction (drug discovery)
  • Recommendation systems
  • Fraud detection in financial networks
  • Knowledge graph completion

For Agentic AI

GNNs enable graph-based reasoning in agents and power knowledge graph completion to discover new relationships.
