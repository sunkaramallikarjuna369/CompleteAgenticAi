# Regulatory Compliance

## EU AI Act
The world's first comprehensive AI regulation:
- **Unacceptable Risk**: Banned (social scoring, real-time biometric surveillance)
- **High Risk**: Strict requirements (healthcare, hiring, credit scoring)
- **Limited Risk**: Transparency obligations (chatbots must disclose they're AI)
- **Minimal Risk**: No restrictions (spam filters, games)

## GDPR & AI
- Right to explanation of automated decisions
- Data minimization applies to training data
- Right to opt out of automated decision-making
- Privacy by design and default

## Industry Regulations
- **Healthcare**: HIPAA (US), MDR (EU)
- **Finance**: SOX, PCI-DSS, Basel III
- **Government**: FedRAMP, FISMA

## For Architects
- Regulatory compliance must be designed in from the start
- Document everything: data sources, model decisions, safety measures
- Consult legal counsel for industry-specific requirements
