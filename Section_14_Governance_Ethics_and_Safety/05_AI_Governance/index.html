Governance Maturity

LevelNameDescription
1Ad HocNo formal governance
2EmergingBasic policies, some oversight
3DefinedFormal framework, clear roles
4ManagedMetrics-driven, continuous improvement
5OptimizedIndustry-leading, predictive

For Architects

Governance is the foundation for enterprise AI adoption. Without it, AI projects face regulatory and reputational risk. Start at Level 2 and work toward Level 4.
