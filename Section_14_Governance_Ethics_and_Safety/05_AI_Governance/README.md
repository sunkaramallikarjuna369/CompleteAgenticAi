# AI Governance

## What is AI Governance?
The organizational framework for managing AI systems responsibly: policies, processes, roles, and oversight.

## Governance Components
### Organizational Structure
- AI Ethics Board/Committee
- Chief AI Officer (CAIO)
- AI Risk Management team
- Cross-functional review processes

### Policies
- Acceptable use policies for AI
- Data governance policies
- Model development guidelines
- Deployment approval processes
- Incident response procedures

### Risk Management
- AI risk taxonomy (technical, ethical, legal, reputational)
- Risk assessment frameworks
- Continuous monitoring
- Regular audits

### Documentation
- Model cards for every deployed model
- Data sheets for training data
- Decision logs for AI choices
- Incident reports

## Maturity Levels
1. **Ad Hoc**: No formal governance
2. **Emerging**: Basic policies, some oversight
3. **Defined**: Formal framework, clear roles
4. **Managed**: Metrics-driven, continuous improvement
5. **Optimized**: Industry-leading, predictive governance

## For Architects
- Governance is the foundation for enterprise AI adoption
- Without governance, AI projects face regulatory and reputational risk
- Start with basic policies and evolve to formal frameworks
