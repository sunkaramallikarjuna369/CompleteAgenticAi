Ethical Principles

Fairness

Avoid bias. Test across demographics. Regular audits.

Transparency

Explain decisions. Disclose AI interaction. Model cards.

Accountability

Clear ownership. Incident response. Appeals process.

Privacy

Minimize data. Anonymize PII. Comply with GDPR/CCPA.

Types of Bias

  • Training Data: Biased data -> biased model
  • Representation: Groups underrepresented in data
  • Measurement: Flawed metrics or proxies
  • Algorithmic: Model amplifies existing biases
  • Deployment: Misuse or misapplication

For Agentic AI

Agents make autonomous decisions at scale. Biased agents can cause real-world harm to many people simultaneously. Ethics are not optional.
