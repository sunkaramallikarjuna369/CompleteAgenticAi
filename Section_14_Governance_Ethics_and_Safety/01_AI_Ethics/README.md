# AI Ethics

## Key Ethical Principles
### Fairness
- Avoid bias in training data and model outputs
- Test for disparate impact across demographic groups
- Regular bias audits

### Transparency
- Explain how AI makes decisions
- Disclose when users are interacting with AI
- Publish model cards and data sheets

### Accountability
- Clear ownership of AI systems
- Incident response procedures
- Appeals processes for AI decisions

### Privacy
- Minimize data collection
- Anonymize personal data
- Comply with privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA)

## Types of Bias
- **Training Data Bias**: Biased data produces biased models
- **Representation Bias**: Underrepresentation of groups
- **Measurement Bias**: Flawed metrics or proxies
- **Algorithmic Bias**: Model amplifies existing biases
- **Deployment Bias**: Misuse or misapplication

## For Agentic AI
- Agents make autonomous decisions - ethics are critical
- Biased agents can cause real-world harm at scale
- Transparency in agent reasoning builds trust
