# Responsible AI

## Responsible AI Principles
1. **Human-Centered**: AI serves humans, not the other way around
2. **Inclusive**: Accessible and beneficial to all
3. **Reliable**: Consistent, predictable behavior
4. **Transparent**: Explainable decisions
5. **Secure**: Protected against misuse
6. **Accountable**: Clear responsibility and oversight

## Frameworks
- Microsoft Responsible AI Standard
- Google AI Principles
- EU Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management System)

## Implementation
- AI Ethics Board: Cross-functional oversight
- Impact Assessments: Evaluate risks before deployment
- Model Cards: Document model capabilities and limitations
- Data Sheets: Document training data sources and biases
- Regular Audits: Ongoing monitoring and evaluation

## For Architects
- Responsible AI is a business requirement, not just ethical
- Build it into the development process from the start
