Principles

Human-Centered

AI serves humans, not the other way around

Inclusive

Accessible and beneficial to all users

Reliable

Consistent and predictable behavior

Transparent

Explainable decisions and processes

Secure

Protected against misuse and attacks

Accountable

Clear responsibility and oversight

Implementation

  • AI Ethics Board: Cross-functional governance
  • Impact Assessments: Evaluate risks before deployment
  • Model Cards: Document capabilities and limitations
  • Regular Audits: Ongoing monitoring and evaluation
  • Feedback Loops: User reporting and issue resolution

For Architects

Responsible AI is a business requirement, not just ethical. Regulators, customers, and partners increasingly demand it.
