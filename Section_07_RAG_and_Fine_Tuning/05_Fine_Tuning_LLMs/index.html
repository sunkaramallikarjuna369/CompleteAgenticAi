Fine-Tuning Methods

Full Fine-Tuning

Update all params. Expensive. Needs lots of data and compute.

LoRA

Add small trainable matrices. 10-100x fewer parameters. Most popular.

QLoRA

LoRA + 4-bit quantization. Fine-tune 65B models on single GPU!

RLHF

Align with human preferences using reward models.

For Agentic AI

LoRA/QLoRA make fine-tuning affordable. RLHF aligns agents with desired behavior. Combine RAG + fine-tuning for best results.
