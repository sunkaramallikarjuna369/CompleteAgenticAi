Vector Databases

Specialized databases for storing and searching high-dimensional vectors (embeddings). They enable semantic search - finding similar meaning, not just matching keywords.

Key Options

DatabaseTypeBest For
PineconeManagedEasy setup, production-ready
WeaviateOpen-sourceHybrid search, flexible
QdrantOpen-sourcePerformance, Rust-based
ChromaOpen-sourceSimple, Python-native
pgvectorExtensionExisting PostgreSQL users

Similarity Metrics

Cosine Similarity

Angle between vectors. Most common for text.

Euclidean Distance

Straight-line distance in vector space.

Dot Product

Magnitude-aware. Good for normalized vectors.
