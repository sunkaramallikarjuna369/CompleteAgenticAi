AI Teams

CrewAI creates multi-agent teams where each agent has a specific role, goal, and backstory. They collaborate to complete complex tasks.

Key Concepts

Agents

Autonomous units with roles, goals, backstories, and tools

Tasks

Specific assignments with descriptions and expected outputs

Crew

A team of agents working together on a mission

Process

Sequential or hierarchical task execution flow
