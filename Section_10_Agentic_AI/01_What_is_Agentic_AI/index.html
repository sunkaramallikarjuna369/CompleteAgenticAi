Defining Agentic AI

AI systems that autonomously pursue goals, make decisions, take actions, and adapt - with minimal human intervention. This is the evolution from chatbots to autonomous agents.

Key Characteristics

Autonomy

Operates independently toward goals

Goal-Directed

Pursues specific objectives

Tool Use

Interacts with external systems

Planning

Decomposes tasks, creates plans

Memory

Maintains context across interactions

Self-Reflection

Evaluates and improves own performance

Levels of Agency

LevelNameCapabilities
L1Simple ChatBasic Q&A, no tools
L2Tool UseCan call functions and APIs
L3PlanningTask decomposition and planning
L4Multi-stepAutonomous multi-step execution
L5Multi-agentCoordinates with other agents
L6Fully AutonomousSelf-improving, minimal oversight

For Architects

Start with L2-L3 agents and evolve. Understanding levels of agency helps scope projects and set realistic expectations.
