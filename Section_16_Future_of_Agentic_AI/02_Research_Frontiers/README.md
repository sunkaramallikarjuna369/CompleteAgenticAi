# Research Frontiers

## World Models
AI systems that build internal models of how the world works, enabling better planning and prediction.

## Constitutional AI
Anthropic's approach: AI systems that follow a set of principles (a "constitution") to self-govern behavior.

## Chain-of-Thought Distillation
Transfer reasoning capabilities from large models to smaller ones.

## Test-Time Compute Scaling
Spend more compute at inference time (thinking longer) rather than training time.

## Tool Creation
Agents that create their own tools when existing tools don't suffice.

## Multi-Agent Learning
Agents that learn to collaborate and specialize through interaction with each other.

## Agentic Reasoning
New reasoning paradigms beyond chain-of-thought: tree search, Monte Carlo methods, debate.

## Long-Horizon Planning
Agents that can plan and execute over days, weeks, or months - not just single sessions.

## For Researchers and Architects
- Stay current with research - the field moves incredibly fast
- Many research ideas become production features within 6-12 months
- Focus on robustness and reliability as capabilities grow
