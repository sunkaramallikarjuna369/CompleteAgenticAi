The Engine of AI Agents

LLMs are massive neural networks trained on internet-scale text. They learn language, reasoning, and knowledge - and serve as the brain of every AI agent.

How LLMs Work

Text -> Tokenizer -> Embeddings -> Transformer Layers x N -> Next Token Probabilities | Sample/Select next token <-----' | Append and repeat until done

Training Pipeline

  • Pre-training: Learn language from massive corpora (months, millions $)
  • Fine-tuning: Adapt to specific tasks/instructions
  • RLHF: Align with human preferences and safety
