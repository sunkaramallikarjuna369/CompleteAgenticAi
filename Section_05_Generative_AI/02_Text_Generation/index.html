How Text Generation Works

Input: "The capital of France is" Step 1: P(next) = {Paris: 0.85, Lyon: 0.05, ...} Step 2: Select "Paris" -> "The capital of France is Paris" Step 3: P(next) = {.: 0.7, ,: 0.2, ...} Repeat until stop condition

Decoding Strategies

Greedy

Always pick highest prob. Fast but repetitive.

Temperature

Scale logits before softmax. Low=focused, high=creative.

Top-k

Sample from top k tokens only. Controls diversity.

Top-p (Nucleus)

Sample from smallest set with cumulative prob >= p.

For Agents

Use low temperature (0.0-0.3) for reliable tool calls and structured output. Higher temperature for creative tasks.
