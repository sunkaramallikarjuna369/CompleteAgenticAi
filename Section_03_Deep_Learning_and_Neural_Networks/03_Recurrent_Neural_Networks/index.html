Why RNNs Were Needed

Standard NNs treat inputs independently, but language and time series require order and context. RNNs maintain hidden state as memory.

Standard NN: x1->[NN]->y1 x2->[NN]->y2 (independent) RNN: x1->[RNN]->h1->[RNN]->h2->[RNN]->h3 (context flows forward)

The Vanishing Gradient Problem

Fatal Flaw

Gradients shrink exponentially through time steps. After 50+ steps, early inputs have zero gradient - the network "forgets".
