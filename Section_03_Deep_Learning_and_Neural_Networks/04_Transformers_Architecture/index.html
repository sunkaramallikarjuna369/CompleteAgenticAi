The Most Important Architecture in AI

Introduced in 2017 by Vaswani et al., the Transformer powers every major LLM: GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Llama.

Why Transformers Won

  • Parallel - all tokens processed simultaneously
  • Long-range attention - any token attends to any other
  • Scalable - predictable improvement with scale
  • Versatile - text, images, audio, code

Self-Attention (QKV)

For "The cat sat on the mat": "sat" attends to: "cat" (0.40), "mat" (0.25), "on" (0.10)... Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k)) * V

Query, Key, Value

  • Query: What am I looking for?
  • Key: What do I contain?
  • Value: What info do I provide?
